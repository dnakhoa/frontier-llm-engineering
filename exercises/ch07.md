## 7.1 🔢 ★ The bandwidth hierarchy

For a cluster of 8-GPU H100 nodes with NVLink inside the node and 400 Gb/s InfiniBand between nodes:

1. Intra-node all-reduce bandwidth per GPU, and inter-node, in GB/s.
2. What is the ratio?
3. A 140 GB gradient all-reduce: how long within a node, and how long across 128 nodes?
4. Given the ratio, state the single most important placement rule for a multi-dimensional parallelism configuration.

## 7.2 🔢 ★★ Topology and the rack boundary

Your scheduler gives you 128 nodes. Two allocations are possible: all 128 in one rack-group behind a single leaf-spine layer, or 128 spread across 16 rack-groups with 2:1 oversubscription at the spine.

1. For a data-parallel all-reduce, how much does the oversubscription cost?
2. For an expert-parallel all-to-all, how much does it cost? Why is the answer different?
3. You cannot choose your allocation. What can you change in your parallelism configuration to compensate?
4. Estimate the throughput difference between the two allocations for the §6.7 configuration.

## 7.3 🔢 ★★ MTBF, properly

1. With a per-GPU MTBF of 40,000 hours, what is the expected time between GPU failures on 4,096 GPUs?
2. Non-GPU components (NIC, cable, PSU, switch, host memory, storage) contribute roughly as many failures again. Revise your estimate.
3. Over a 45-day run, how many interruptions do you expect?
4. Failures are not independent — a rack power event takes out 32 nodes at once. How does that change the *distribution* of downtime, even if it does not change the mean?
5. Which do you engineer against: the mean, or the tail? What does each require?

## 7.4 🏗️ ★★ Diagnose eight cluster symptoms

For each, give the two most likely causes and the first diagnostic you would run.

1. Step time increased 15% and stayed there, no code change.
2. Step time is fine on average but has periodic 40-second spikes.
3. One rank's loss is `NaN`; all others are finite.
4. The job hangs with no error, all GPUs at 100% utilization.
5. Throughput is fine for 20 minutes after every restart, then degrades.
6. NCCL reports a timeout on an all-reduce that has run successfully 400,000 times.
7. Two runs with identical configs and seeds diverge after 1,000 steps.
8. The loss is slightly worse than an earlier identical run, and nothing errors.

Item 8 is the hardest and the most important.

## 7.5 🔢 ★★ Silent data corruption

A cluster has 4,096 GPUs. Published field studies suggest silent data corruption occurs at a rate on the order of one incident per few thousand GPUs per year — take one per 2,000 GPU-years.

1. Over a 60-day run, what is the expected number of SDC incidents?
2. An SDC in a gradient all-reduce corrupts one value on one rank. How does that propagate through the next optimizer step?
3. Why does this not usually produce a `NaN`?
4. Name three detection strategies and rank them by cost.
5. What is the argument for and against checksumming every collective?

## 7.6 🏗️ ★★ Design the on-call

You run a 90-day training job on 2,048 GPUs, costing roughly $150,000 per day.

1. What alerts do you set, and at what thresholds?
2. Which alerts page a human at 3am, and which wait until morning? Justify the line.
3. What should be automated with no human in the loop?
4. What is your runbook for "job died, cause unknown"?
5. How do you avoid the failure mode where the automation restarts a job that is silently broken?

## 7.7 🔢 ★★ The cost of a hang

Your job hangs rather than crashing. Detection is by a watchdog on step-time.

1. With a watchdog threshold of 10× the median step time and a 10-second step, how long until detection?
2. Add 4 minutes to reschedule and 6 minutes to reload an 840 GB checkpoint. Total cost per hang?
3. At $150,000/day on 2,048 GPUs, what does one hang cost?
4. You could set the watchdog to 3× instead of 10×. What is the risk, and how would you decide?
