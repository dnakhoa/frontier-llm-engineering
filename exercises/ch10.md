## 10.1 🏗️ ★★★ Reconstruct the run

Without looking at Chapter 10, write the full DeepSeek-V3 recipe. Cover:

1. Model: total and active parameters, layers, hidden dimension, attention variant with its parameters, MoE structure, and any auxiliary objectives.
2. Data: token count, mixture shape, tokenizer.
3. Parallelism: every degree, on how many GPUs, and which axes are intra-node.
4. Precision: what is FP8, what is BF16, what is FP32, and the scaling strategy.
5. Optimizer and schedule.
6. Context length, and how it got there.
7. Cost, and what the cost figure covers.

Then check. Score yourself on which of the seven you could produce, and reread the corresponding chapter for anything you missed.

## 10.2 🔢 ★★ Which choice bought the most

Rank DeepSeek-V3's four headline choices — MLA, fine-grained MoE with auxiliary-loss-free balancing, FP8, DualPipe — by their contribution to the low training cost. Support each with arithmetic.

1. Estimate the training-cost multiple that each choice saved, holding the others fixed.
2. Which two, if removed, would most change the story?
3. Which one is most about *serving* rather than training, and how much does that change the ranking if you care about total lifetime cost?
4. Which is most specific to their hardware constraints and least likely to transfer?

## 10.3 🔢 ★★ Price the alternative

Construct a hypothetical "DeepSeek-V3 as a Western lab would have built it in 2024": dense 400B, BF16, GQA, standard 3D parallelism.

1. Estimate the training FLOPs for the same 14.8T tokens.
2. At H100 pricing and a realistic dense MFU, estimate GPU-hours and dollar cost.
3. What is the ratio to V3's reported figure?
4. How much of the difference is MoE, and how much is FP8?
5. Is the resulting model better, worse, or comparable? What would you need to know to answer that?

## 10.4 🏗️ ★★ The auxiliary-loss-free scheme

1. State the standard MoE auxiliary loss and what it does to the objective.
2. State DeepSeek's bias-based alternative precisely enough to implement.
3. Why is the bias update not part of the gradient?
4. What does the bias approach fail to do that an auxiliary loss does?
5. Design the experiment that would convince you the bias approach is better. What result would falsify the claim?

## 10.5 🔢 ★★ Multi-token prediction

V3 uses MTP depth 1: one sequential MTP module, with loss weight 0.3 (0.1 for the last 4.8T tokens).

1. What does MTP add to the training objective, mechanically?
2. What is the parameter and compute overhead of the MTP module? (Check your estimate against the released checkpoint's 685B.)
3. Name two distinct benefits claimed for MTP, one about training and one about inference.
4. Why weight 0.3 rather than 1.0? What would you expect at 1.0?
5. The MTP module can be discarded for standard inference. Under what inference scheme is it kept, and why?

## 10.7 🏗️ ★★★ What would you change
