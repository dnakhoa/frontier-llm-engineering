## 13.1 🔢 ★ The four-model budget

PPO on a 70B policy, 70B value model, 70B frozen reference, 7B frozen reward model. Mixed precision, AdamW.

1. Static memory for each of the four, and the total.
2. How many 80 GB H100s for the static state alone?
3. Repeat with the value model shrunk to 7B.
4. Repeat under RLOO (no value model).
5. Express RLOO's saving as a percentage and say which of PPO's problems it removes besides memory.

## 13.2 🔢 ★★ Where the wall-clock goes

One PPO iteration: 512 prompts, 1 rollout each, mean 600 generated tokens. Policy is 70B. Generation runs at 2,400 tokens/s/GPU on 32 GPUs; training runs at 8,000 tokens/s/GPU on the same 32.

1. Time to generate the rollouts.
2. Time for the reward model and reference forward passes, assuming each is ~1/8 the cost of a training step on the same tokens.
3. Time for 4 PPO epochs of training on those tokens.
4. What fraction of the iteration is generation?
5. You switch from `model.generate()` to vLLM and generation throughput rises 8×. What is the new fraction, and what is now the bottleneck?

## 13.3 🔢 ★★ Baselines and variance

For one prompt you sample 8 responses with rewards: `[2.1, 2.3, 1.9, 2.2, 2.0, 3.4, 2.1, 2.2]`.

1. Compute the REINFORCE gradient weights with no baseline.
2. Compute RLOO's leave-one-out advantages.
3. Compute GRPO's group-mean advantages, with and without dividing by the group standard deviation.
4. Which sample gets the largest positive weight under each scheme?
5. Explain, using these numbers, why the no-baseline version has ruinous variance.
6. Now all eight rewards are `[0, 0, 0, 0, 0, 0, 0, 0]`. What are the advantages under RLOO and under GRPO with std normalization? What goes wrong?

## 13.4 🔢 ★★ The KL budget

Your reward has $\sigma = 1.0$. You run PPO with $\beta = 0.02$ and observe KL rising 0.4 nats per 100 steps, reward rising 0.05 per 100 steps.

1. At this rate, what KL do you reach after 2,000 steps?
2. From [Exercise 12.4](ch12.md) part 5, your best-of-$n$ diagnostic suggested a safe budget of 1.8 nats. At what step do you cross it?
3. Using the adaptive controller in §13.6, what should $\beta$ become to hold KL at 1.8?
4. Reward is still rising at the point you hit the budget. Argue for stopping anyway.
5. What independent measurement would change your mind?

## 13.6 🏗️ ★★ Choose the method

For each situation, choose among rejection sampling, DPO, PPO, RLOO, and GRPO, and justify in two sentences.

1. Math problems with an answer key, 100k prompts, 64 GPUs.
2. General helpfulness, a good reward model, 512 GPUs, an experienced RL team.
3. General helpfulness, preference pairs from a public dataset, 8 GPUs, two engineers.
4. Code generation with unit tests, and you need it working in a week.
5. Safety refusals, where you must be able to state exactly how far the model moved.
