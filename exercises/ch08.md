## 8.1 🔢 ★ Chinchilla arithmetic

You have a compute budget of $1 \times 10^{24}$ FLOPs. Use $C = 6ND$ and the Chinchilla ratio of ~20 tokens per parameter.

1. What is the compute-optimal model size $N$?
2. What is the corresponding token count $D$?
3. Now you learn the model will serve 10 billion requests over its lifetime, each generating 500 tokens. Estimate the inference FLOPs and compare to the training budget.
4. Given part 3, argue for a model smaller than Chinchilla-optimal. Roughly how much smaller?
5. Llama-3-8B was trained on ~15T tokens. What tokens-per-parameter ratio is that, and what does it say about Meta's view of parts 3 and 4?

## 8.2 🔢 ★★ The learning-rate schedule

A run uses peak LR $3\times10^{-4}$, linear warmup over 2,000 steps, then cosine decay to 10% of peak over 500,000 total steps.

1. What is the LR at step 1,000? At step 2,000? At step 250,000? At step 500,000?
2. Sketch the shape. Why is warmup there at all — what specifically goes wrong without it?
3. You need to extend the run to 700,000 steps after it has already started. What breaks about the cosine schedule, and what are your options?
4. Why do some labs use a constant-then-decay (trapezoidal / WSD) schedule instead? What does it buy?

## 8.3 🔢 ★★ Batch size and the LR coupling

Your run uses global batch 4M tokens with peak LR $3\times10^{-4}$. You double the batch to 8M tokens.

1. Under the square-root scaling heuristic, what LR would you use?
2. Under linear scaling?
3. Which is more appropriate for Adam, and why?
4. What is the "critical batch size," and what happens above it?
5. You have 2× the GPUs and want 2× throughput. Explain why simply doubling the batch may not give 2× the progress per unit time.

## 8.4 🔢 ★★ Precision and dynamic range

1. BF16 has 8 exponent bits and 7 mantissa bits. FP16 has 5 and 10. State the approximate max representable magnitude and the relative precision of each.
2. Gradients during training commonly reach $10^{-8}$ or smaller. Which format handles this without loss scaling, and why?
3. FP8 E4M3 has 4 exponent bits and 3 mantissa bits. What is its dynamic range, and why is per-tensor scaling insufficient?
4. Summing 8,192 BF16 values in a BF16 accumulator: estimate the relative error, and explain why FP32 accumulation fixes it.
5. DeepSeek-V3 kept certain operations in BF16 despite training in FP8. Name three you would expect to be on that list, with reasons.

## 8.5 🔢 ★★ Read the loss curve

For each pattern, give the two most likely causes and the first thing you would change.

1. Loss decreases smoothly, then jumps by 0.4 at step 47,000 and does not recover.
2. Loss decreases, spikes, and recovers within 200 steps — repeatedly, every few thousand steps.
3. Loss plateaus at a value clearly above the expected curve, from very early.
4. Loss is fine but gradient norm grows steadily over the run.
5. Loss decreases faster than a previous identical-config run, then ends worse.
6. Loss is `NaN` at step 3.

## 8.6 🏗️ ★★ Design a recipe

Write the full optimization recipe for a 30B MoE on 6T tokens, and justify every number.

1. Optimizer and its hyperparameters ($\beta_1$, $\beta_2$, $\epsilon$, weight decay).
2. Peak LR, warmup length, decay shape, final LR.
3. Global batch size in tokens, and how it ramps (if it does).
4. Gradient clipping threshold.
5. Precision plan — what is in FP8, what is in BF16, what is in FP32.
6. Stability measures (z-loss, QK-norm, anything else) and whether you would use them from step 0 or hold them in reserve.
7. What you monitor, and the threshold at which you intervene.
