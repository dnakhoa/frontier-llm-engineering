## 21.4 🔢 ★★★ The silent data-loader bug

A 60-day run on 2,048 GPUs is interrupted 180 times. Each time, the data loader restarts from the beginning of the corpus while the model and optimizer resume correctly. The corpus is 8T tokens and the run consumes 15T tokens.

1. Assume interruptions are uniformly distributed in time. Roughly how many times is the first 1% of the corpus seen?
2. How many times is the last 20% seen?
3. What does the loss curve look like, and why does it look *better* than a correct run?
4. What is the effect on the final model?
5. Write the test that catches this in under a minute of runtime.

## 21.5 🏗️ ★★ Design the checkpoint system

For a 200B MoE on 1,024 GPUs, 45-day run.

1. Sharded or consolidated? Justify with the arithmetic.
2. Synchronous or asynchronous write? What does async require?
3. Checkpoint cadence and retention policy. How much storage does the policy need?
4. How do you make the write atomic, and what does a partial write look like to the loader?
5. You want to reshard from 1,024 to 512 GPUs for a debugging run. What must the format record?
6. What do you validate after every automatic restart, and why?

## 21.8 🧪 ★★ Break the checkpoint lab

Run `lab21_checkpoint_resume`.
