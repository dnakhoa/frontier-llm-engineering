## 9.1 🔢 ★ The cost of long context

A 30B dense model, 60 layers, $d = 6144$, GQA with 8 KV heads and head dim 128.

1. Attention FLOPs per layer scale as $O(s^2)$. Compute the attention FLOPs for one sequence at 8K, 32K, and 128K.
2. The FFN FLOPs scale as $O(s)$. Compute those at each length.
3. At which sequence length does attention overtake the FFN as the dominant cost?
4. Total FLOPs to train on 5B tokens at 128K context versus at 8K. What is the ratio?

## 9.2 🔢 ★★ Budget the mid-training phase

Your bulk pre-training run was 6T tokens at 8K context. You want to extend to 128K.

1. Using the progressive recipe from §9.3 (8K → 32K → 128K), propose a token budget for each stage and justify it.
2. Using your §9.1 answers, what is the compute cost of your mid-training phase as a fraction of the bulk run?
3. If you instead trained the whole 6T at 128K, what would the run have cost? Express as a multiple.
4. State the case for mid-training in one sentence, using the number from part 3.

## 9.3 🔢 ★★ RoPE extension arithmetic

A model trained at 8K with RoPE base $\theta = 10{,}000$, head dim 128.

1. From [Exercise 5.5](ch05.md), roughly how many dimension pairs are out of distribution at 128K?
2. **Position Interpolation** scales positions by $L_{\text{train}}/L_{\text{target}}$. What scale factor is that here, and what does it do to the *effective resolution* at short distances?
3. **NTK-aware scaling** adjusts $\theta$ instead. What new $\theta$ would keep the slowest wavelength proportional to the new context?
4. **YaRN** applies different treatment per dimension. Which dimensions does it interpolate, which does it leave alone, and why is that better than either extreme?
5. Rank the three by expected quality at 128K and by expected quality retained at 8K.

## 9.5 🔢 ★★ Long-context evaluation

You extend a model to 128K and it scores 99% on needle-in-a-haystack at all depths.

1. Why is that not evidence the model works at 128K?
2. RULER [\[49\]](../book/appendix/b-references.md#49-ruler) reports "effective context length" often being a quarter to a half of the advertised length. Design a test that would reveal that gap for your model.
3. Name four RULER-style task categories that are harder than single-needle retrieval, in increasing difficulty.
4. Your model's effective context measures at 32K against an advertised 128K. What do you report, and to whom?

## 9.6 🏗️ ★★ Diagnose six mid-training failures

For each, name the two most likely causes and the first check.

1. 8K perplexity got 8% worse after extension, and 128K retrieval is good.
2. 128K perplexity is fine, but 128K reasoning tasks are much worse than 8K reasoning tasks.
3. Training at 32K works; training at 128K OOMs at the same micro-batch.
4. Long-context extension worked in a small-scale test and failed at 30B.
5. The model handles 100K well and collapses past 120K.
6. Throughput at 128K is 40× worse than at 8K, not the ~16× your §9.1 arithmetic predicted.
