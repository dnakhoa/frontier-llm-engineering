# Exercises — Chapter 24: Translating frontier JDs

Covers [Chapter 24](../book/part-5-the-job/24-translating-jds.md). Solutions: [`solutions/ch24.md`](solutions/ch24.md).

Legend: 🔢 drill · 🏗️ design · 📄 paper · ★ direct · ★★ synthesis · ★★★ beyond the chapter

Most of this set has no single right answer. The solutions give worked examples and the reasoning; yours will differ and should.

---

## 24.1 🏗️ ★ Decode three real JDs

Find three current frontier-lab job descriptions — Anthropic, OpenAI, Google DeepMind, Meta, xAI, Alibaba/Qwen, DeepSeek, Moonshot, Mistral, AI2, or a cloud/hardware vendor. For each:

1. Which chapters of this book is it describing? Use §24.2's decoder ring.
2. Which archetype from §24.3 is it closest to?
3. What is the team currently struggling with? Point at the specific sentence that tells you.
4. What would they test in an interview, given the JD's emphasis?
5. What percentage of the listed requirements do you currently meet, honestly?

---

## 24.2 🏗️ ★★ The self-assessment

Pick one target role.

1. List every requirement from a real JD for it.
2. Score yourself ✅ / 🟡 / ❌ on each.
3. Separate the ❌ items into "learnable in a month," "learnable in six months," and "requires a job that has one."
4. For the third category, what is the closest substitute you can demonstrate?
5. Which single gap, if closed, moves the most others?

---

## 24.3 🏗️ ★★ Answer the four interview questions

For your target role, write out full answers.

1. The arithmetic question. Pick the one from §24.3's archetype and answer it on paper, showing every step.
2. The design question. Write the *questions you would ask first*, then a configuration with justification.
3. The debugging scenario. Give an ordered list of hypotheses with the cheapest test for each.
4. The depth question. Pick the thing on your résumé you know best and write out three levels of follow-up.

For part 3, use one of: "loss spiked at step 40,000," "throughput dropped 30%," or "works in eval, worse in production."

---

## 24.4 🏗️ ★★ Design a portfolio project

You have one month and no cluster.

1. Pick the gap you most need to demonstrate.
2. Design a project that produces reviewable evidence of it. Be specific about the artifact.
3. What is the honest failure you expect, and how will you write it up?
4. Where does it go — a repo, a post, a PR to an open project?
5. §24.6 ranks an open-source contribution highest. Find one concrete issue in vLLM, `trl`, `torchtitan`, `lm-evaluation-harness`, or FlashAttention that matches your gap. What is it?

## 24.5 🔢 ★★ The arithmetic drill set

These are the calculations §24.5 says every archetype's interview contains. Do them from memory, then check.

1. Memory to train a 70B dense model in mixed precision.
2. KV cache per token for a model with 80 layers, 8 KV heads, head dim 128, BF16.
3. Arithmetic intensity of RMSNorm on an $(8192, 8192)$ BF16 tensor, and whether it is compute- or memory-bound.
4. Tokens/second needed to train on 15T tokens in 60 days.
5. Time per output token for a 70B BF16 model at batch 1 on an H100.
6. GPU-hours for a rejection-sampling round: 200k prompts, $k=8$, 600 tokens, 2,400 tok/s/GPU.
7. Optimal checkpoint interval at $C = 10$ s, MTBF = 3 h.
8. Compute-optimal model size for $10^{24}$ FLOPs under Chinchilla.

## 24.6 🏗️ ★★★ The honest gap analysis

1. Write down what you would be hired to do *tomorrow*, based on what you have actually shipped — not what you are interested in.
2. Write down your target role.
3. What is the shortest credible path between them? Name the intermediate role if there is one.
4. §25.4 says internal transitions are far easier than external ones. Does that change your path?
5. What is the single most likely reason you will not close this gap, and what would prevent it?
