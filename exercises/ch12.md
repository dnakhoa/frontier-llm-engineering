## 12.1 🔢 ★ The agreement ceiling

Human annotators agree with each other 75% of the time on general helpfulness comparisons.

1. Your reward model achieves 74% accuracy on held-out human preferences. Is it good?
2. A colleague improves it to 84%. Give two explanations, one good and one bad.
3. How would you distinguish between them, using data you already have?
4. What accuracy would a *perfect* reward model achieve on this data? Explain.

## 12.2 🔢 ★★ Stratify the accuracy

Your annotation interface collects preference strength. Held-out accuracy by stratum:

| Strength | Fraction of pairs | RM accuracy |

| Significantly better | 20% | 91% |
| Better | 30% | 82% |
| Slightly better | 35% | 68% |
| Negligibly better | 15% | 53% |

1. What is the overall accuracy?
2. Which stratum should worry you, and which should not?

4. What does the "negligibly better" row tell you about the ceiling from §12.1?

## 12.3 🔢 ★★ Length bias, quantified

Your reward model's scores have Spearman correlation 0.62 with response length on held-out data.

1. What fraction of the reward's variance is explained by length alone, approximately?
2. During RLHF, mean response length grows from 280 to 520 tokens over 2,000 steps. Reward rose by 0.8 (in RM units, $\sigma = 1.0$). How much of that gain is attributable to length?
3. Design a control that isolates genuine quality improvement from length inflation.
4. Name three mitigations and rank them by how much they cost you elsewhere.

## 12.4 🏗️ ★★ Best-of-$n$ as a diagnostic

You have two reward models. Sampling $n$ responses and selecting each RM's favourite, then judging with an independent evaluator, gives:

| $n$ | RM-A win rate | RM-B win rate |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 50% | 50% |
| 4 | 61% | 63% |
| 16 | 68% | 71% |
| 64 | 73% | 69% |
| 256 | 76% | 61% |

1. Which reward model would you ship, and why?
2. What is happening to RM-B at large $n$?
3. Predict what happens if you run PPO against each. Which fails first?
4. Why is this test cheaper and more informative than running RL to find out?
5. At what $n$ would you stop trusting RM-B, and what does that imply about a safe KL budget?
